About Crumb & Co.

Welcome to our bakery! We are passionate about creating delicious and visually stunning cakes for all occasions. Our team of skilled bakers and decorators work tirelessly to bring your cake dreams to life. From custom designs to classic flavors, we strive to exceed your expectations and make every celebration memorable.



Brian's Story

Crumb & Co. was founded in 2024 by Mr. Brian Mbuyezi in his hometown of Cape Town, after he retired as an executive chef to pursue his dream of becoming a bakery store owner. Brian first began learning to bake in his kitchen at his home in Johannesburg. He later shared his baked cakes with friends who were professional bakers, and their encouragement inspired him to finally open the bakery he had long dreamed of.



Brian's personal quote and reason for starting the business

"I love cake — it's the best thing I've ever tasted. So, I decided, why not make a cake bakery for lovers of cake, such as I am?"

Vision Statement

"Mr. Brian Mbuyezi's Crumb & Co. is a bakery shop that focuses exclusively on selling cakes. As Brian puts it: 'I love cake — it's the best thing I've ever tasted. So, I decided, why not make a cake bakery for lovers of cake, such as I am?' Mr. Brian Mbuyezi aims to make his bakery an international hotspot for cake lovers such as himself. Brian's short-term goal is to first make his bakery shop known nationally, before further transitioning it into an international cake bakery. Brian also aims to build a diverse community by sharing his baking journey with fellow cake lovers, along with the cakes he makes and the ingredients used — because, in his own words, sharing this shows his love and passion for the line of work he is currently doing"
