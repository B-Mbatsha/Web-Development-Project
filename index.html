Welcome


Made with love and passion: What started as a passion project soon grew into a calling, and that calling eventually found its true home in Cape Town, where Crumb&Co bakes today. Every cake that leaves our kitchen is handcrafted from scratch, using real butter, real flour, and real care — never shortcuts, never mixes. We believe a cake should do more than look beautiful, it should taste like the moment it was made for, whether that's a wedding, a milestone birthday, or simply a Tuesday that deserves celebrating. It's this blend of fine-dining discipline and genuine, home-grown love that makes every slice unmistakably Crumb&Co.
