Why Choose Us?

At Crumb & Co., every cake is crafted with the same precision and passion Brian brought to fine dining as an executive chef — now poured entirely into the art of baking. We believe great cake starts with quality ingredients, honest craftsmanship, and a genuine love for what we do. Unlike mass-produced bakeries, every order is made with care, attention to detail, and a personal touch that reflects Brian's own journey — from a home kitchen in Johannesburg to a dream realized in Cape Town. When you choose Crumb & Co., you're not just buying a cake — you're supporting a story of passion, dedication, and a commitment to bringing a little more sweetness into your celebrations.
